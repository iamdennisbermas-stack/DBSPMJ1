# KnoticaPMJ - CRM & Project Management Journey Automation

Welcome to **KnoticaPMJ**, the DevOps-driven CRM automation architecture for Knotica Solutions Inc.

## Architecture & Workflows
- **Stage 1 (SALES)**:
  - `SALESFORCE`: Masterfile and schema for internal sales executives.
  - `LEAD`: Customer prospect management.
  - `STD_SALES_PITCH`: Template logic and pitch message handling.
  - `SALES_PIPELINE`: Automated escalation triggering pre-consultation creation.
- **Stage 2 (CONSULT)**:
  - `PRECON`: Pre-consultation records and event-driven lifecycle management (`Submitted`, `Suspended`, `Cancelled`, `Resumed`).
  - `CONSULTANT`: Professional consultants directory.
  - `PRECON_MAIN_CATEGORY` & `PRECON_SUB_CATEGORY`: Taxonomy management enforcing strict category rules.

## Setup & Secrets Configuration
Configure the following **GitHub Actions Secrets** in your repository (`Settings > Secrets and variables > Actions`):
1. `SMTP_PASSWORD`: Password for `info@knoticasolutions.com` Office365 account.
2. `WEBHOOK_URL`: Slack or Microsoft Teams incoming webhook URL for staff notifications.
